# Privacy Policy

**BIOGLOW™ Scorer**, made by kita 39112. Last updated October 25th, 2026.

The app is a robot game scorer. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no
third-party trackers, and it never asks for your name, email address, photos,
contacts or location.

## What stays on your device

- **Saved matches** — team number, round, mission scores and the match log, kept
  in the app's own database on the device and removed when you delete the app.
- **Settings** — sounds, theme, the preserved team number and the counters shown
  in the app, kept in the app's preferences on the device.

None of this is uploaded to us, and we have no server that could receive it.

## What leaves your device

- **Rule updates.** When you tap "Check for Rule Updates", the app downloads a
  scoring file from GitHub. GitHub receives the request, including your IP
  address, as it does for any web request; it is handled under
  [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
  Nothing about your matches is sent with the request.
- **Anonymous usage events**, if the build you have was compiled with an
  analytics key. The app then reports when it launches, which of the four tools
  you opened, and, when a scoring session ends, how many missions were scored,
  how many precision tokens were left and the total score. These events carry
  the app version, the operating system version, the device model and the
  country derived from the IP address, which is not stored. They carry no
  identifier that can be traced back to you or your device, and no team numbers.
  They are processed by [Aptabase](https://aptabase.com/privacy-policy).

## Tracking

The app does not track you. Nothing it collects is linked to your identity or
shared with data brokers, advertisers or any other third party, and nothing is
sold.

## Children

The app is made for *FIRST*® LEGO® League teams, including students under 13.
It collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.
